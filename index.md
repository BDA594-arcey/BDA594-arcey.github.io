# WEB-01 Assignment

Dataset: UCI Machine learning Repository, Online Retail II  
https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii 

This dataset is used for studying retail operations and consumer purchasing behaviors. The Online Retail II data contains real transactions from UK within a 2 year period. The date includes product, invoices and prices which provides a wide use of consumer behavior.

Potential Applications: Sales Demand, Forecasting, Customer profiling, Pricing Analysis

---

Book: Think Stats (V.2)  
https://greenteapress.com/thinkstats2/thinkstats2.pdf

This book gives a simple introduction to tools used in data analysis. Follows the basic steps of starting a dataset, first cleaning the data and then look at each variable. It walks thru the steps of finding relationships between variable and correlations and teaches how to hypothesize, measure and test. The final lesson is the visualization which is important to explain or communicate the final results.

I selected this book for it simplicity as I am in the beginning stages of learning how to extract and compile large data to analyze. I thought this was an easy, informative book that can teach me the fundamentals of datasets while using python which I'm very interested in.
